# Bibliography and Citations

The mathematical foundations, constitutive formulations, and numerical techniques in `Erebus.jl` build upon key literature in numerical geodynamics, hydrogeology, and planetary science.

---

## Primary References

- **Gerya, T. (2019)**. *Introduction to Numerical Geodynamic Modelling* (2nd ed.). Cambridge University Press.  
  [https://doi.org/10.1017/9781316534243](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781316534243)  
  *Foundational reference for the staggered-grid finite-difference formulation, Marker-in-Cell (MIC) advection, and coupled two-phase fluid-matrix percolation.*

- **Terzaghi, K. (1925)**. *Erdbaumechanik auf bodenphysikalischer Grundlage*. Franz Deuticke, Leipzig.  
  *Seminal formulation of effective stress theory in porous media and analytical one-dimensional consolidation.*

- **Wang, H. F. (2000)**. *Theory of Linear Poroelasticity with Applications to Geomechanics and Hydrogeology*. Princeton University Press.  
  *Mathematical formulation of drained compressibility, Biot-Willis coupling, and Skempton coefficients.*

- **Lichtenberg, T., Golabek, G. J., Burn, R., Meyer, M. R., Alibert, Y., Gerya, T. V., & Mordasini, C. (2019)**. A water budget divide in planetesimals from Aluminum-26 heating. *Nature Astronomy*, 3(4), 307-313.  
  [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41550-018-0688-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41550-018-0688-5)  
  *Radionuclide heating budgets, dehydration kinetics, and volatile evolution in early Solar System planetesimals.*

- **Lichtenberg, T., Bower, D. J., Hammond, M., Boukrouche, R., Sanan, P., Tsai, S.-M., & Pierrehumbert, R. T. (2021)**. Vertically resolved magma ocean-protoatmosphere evolution: setting the stage for primary atmospheres. *Journal of Geophysical Research: Planets*, 126(2), e2020JE006711.  
  [https://doi.org/10.1029/2020JE006711](https://doi.org/10.1029/2020JE006711)
